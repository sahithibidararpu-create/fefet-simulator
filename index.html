01 — WORKING PRINCIPLE

How FeFET Works

A FeFET integrates a ferroelectric layer into the gate stack of a conventional MOSFET. The spontaneous polarization of this layer shifts the threshold voltage, enabling non-volatile binary memory storage.

01
⬡

Ferroelectric Layer

Hafnium Zirconium Oxide (HZO) or BaTiO₃ acts as the gate insulator. Its crystal lattice can maintain two stable polarization states without an applied field — the foundation of non-volatility.

02
↕

Polarization Switching

Applying a voltage pulse above the coercive field (E_c) flips the dipole alignment. Polarization UP (P↑) and DOWN (P↓) represent the two memory states — analogous to "0" and "1".

03
Δ

Threshold Voltage Shift

Each polarization state induces a different charge distribution at the semiconductor interface, shifting V_th by ΔV_th = qP_r/C_ox. This memory window separates the two states during a read operation.

04
⊙

Non-Destructive Read

Unlike FeRAM, FeFET reads are non-destructive. A read voltage between the two V_th values produces a high or low current, distinguishing states without disturbing the polarization.
